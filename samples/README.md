Structures built into profold as examples.

- `caffeine.sdf` — caffeine, PubChem CID 2519 3D conformer.
- `c60.xyz` — buckminsterfullerene, generated from the truncated icosahedron.
- `1crn.pdb.gz` — crambin, PDB entry 1CRN (RCSB PDB).
- `1ubq.pdb.gz` — ubiquitin, PDB entry 1UBQ (RCSB PDB).
- `1ema.pdb.gz` — green fluorescent protein, PDB entry 1EMA (RCSB PDB).
- `4hhb.pdb.gz` — deoxyhaemoglobin, PDB entry 4HHB (RCSB PDB).
- `9sv9.pdb.gz` — *E. coli* S-adenosylmethionine synthetase (I103A) with
  (S,S)-SAM and PPNP, PDB entry 9SV9 (RCSB PDB); ANISOU records removed.
- `rocksalt.cif` — halite (NaCl), Fm-3m, a = 5.6402 Å: two sites and the
  face-centring operations.
- `silicon.vasp` — diamond-cubic silicon, a = 5.431 Å, 3×3×3 supercell.
- `lj-melt.lammpstrj.gz` — 500 Lennard-Jones atoms in reduced units, an FCC
  crystal at ρ = 1.0 heated from T = 0.3 to 2.0 over 2,400 velocity-Verlet steps
  until it melts; every 60th step kept. Simulated for this file.
